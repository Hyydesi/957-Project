Echo Verse is an innovative social networking platform designed for the Web3 era, where users can connect, create, and interact within a decentralized, open environment. Our mission is to redefine the social experience, offering an immersive space where individuals can engage with one another, share unique moments through video, and take part in exciting quests. Echo Verse empowers creators, idols, and fans to build deeper, more meaningful relationships in the digital world, providing a truly next-generation platform across PWA web, Android, and iOS.

Learning from Web2

We researched many Web3 social networking apps, but they are quite rare, and their interface and experience are not very good. Therefore, we studied the UX of popular global Web2 social apps like Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Snapchat, WeVerse, and more.

After that, we began creating wireframes and held meetings with managers and stakeholders to discuss features, sitemap, user flow, and timelines for each phase.

From Dark to Light

We first planned a dark mode. But for a social app aimed at a younger audience, light mode made everything clearer and more dynamic.

And indeed, it turned out to be the right choice — everything became brighter and more engaging.

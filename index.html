We are a design agency helping teams design, build, and scale digital products that actually work and drive real impact.

957 is a concept-driven design studio founded in 2025 and based in Vietnam. Rooted in modernist design principles and graphic design tradition, our work combines typography, grid systems and motion to create digital experiences that balance clarity, usability and bold visual expression. Our team remains intentionally small, under 10 people, which allows creative direction to stay personal and decisions to stay sharp. Every project is led closely by the founders. No layers. No dilution.

    (Process)5 steps

    From first hello to launch day

    The same five steps, every project. One small team from the first message to the last file, with a founder at the table the whole way through.

    1. [01] Say hello Day 0
      11

      Tell us what you're building

      Send a note through the form, book a 15-minute call, or write to ninefiveseven@vn.com. A few lines on the product, the people it's for and the deadline are enough to start.

      A founder reads every message and replies within one working day. If we're not the right studio for it, we'll say so, and point you to someone who is.

      You bring
      An idea, a brief or a problem
      We reply
      Within 24 hours

      Queued

    2. [02] Align Days 1–3
      22

      Find the real problem

      A 45-minute call with the founders. We ask about goals, users, competitors and constraints, and we listen far more than we pitch.

      Afterwards you get a short discovery note: what we heard, what we think the real problem is, and the questions still open. Everything after is built on it.

      Format
      Video call, 45 min
      You get
      A discovery note

      Queued

    3. [03] Scope Days 3–5
      33

      One fixed scope, in writing

      We turn the note into a proposal: deliverables, milestones, a timeline and one fixed price. No hourly surprises, no vague phases.

      Once it's signed and the deposit lands, your slot is booked in the studio calendar and a kickoff pack lists everything we need from your side.

      You get
      Proposal and timeline
      To start
      50% deposit

      Queued

    4. [04] Design Weeks 2–6
      44

      Concept first, then craft

      We open with two or three concept directions, each with its own idea, type and grid. You choose one, and we push it all the way through.

      Weekly check-ins, one shared Figma file and two rounds of revisions per milestone. You see the work as it grows, never only at the reveal.

      Check-ins
      Every week
      Revisions
      2 rounds per milestone

      Queued

    5. [05] Launch Week 6+
      55

      Ship it, hand it over, stay close

      Final files, guidelines and motion specs, or a live site built with our dev partners. Clean, named and ready for your team to run.

      After launch we stay on for 30 days to fix, tune and answer questions. Most clients come back for the next chapter.

      Handover
      Files, guidelines, code
      Support
      30 days after launch

      Queued
